\subsection{Discussion}
The pattern of results matches the design. Relevancy is where the two pipelines differ most by construction: \base is asked to describe the image in context, whereas \sys is asked to describe the \emph{product} in context, so scene elements that \base reports correctly are counted as irrelevant here. Efficiency is not significant for a related reason: the completeness validator deliberately adds coverage, which lengthens \sys texts, and per-image efficiency varies widely with product complexity (\sys was higher on 13 products and \base on 14). The ablation also points to a concrete improvement. Removing completeness at fusion lowers the post-hoc avoidable-redundancy rate from 0.178 to 0.045, which indicates that many completeness additions (e.g., sleeve length) are judged to repeat attributes that are already on the page. Tightening the completeness validator's DOM check is therefore the most direct way to improve \sys's redundancy score without sacrificing coverage.
