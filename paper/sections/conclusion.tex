\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
We presented \sys, a gap-aware alt-text pipeline that treats the product page as known context and uses claim-level validation to write concise, purchase-relevant descriptions for BLV shoppers. With the underlying model held constant, \sys produced significantly more relevant alt text than a strong context-aware baseline, with consistent trends in objectivity and relevant novelty. The ablation shows that each validator contributes something distinct: coverage from completeness, factual correction from accuracy, and less DOM repetition from redundancy. These findings are preliminary. The test set covers 30 images from one retailer and two product families, and all judgments are automated and rubric-based rather than drawn from BLV users. Future work will (i) run a user study with BLV participants to test whether higher rubric scores translate into better shopping decisions, (ii) add an independent factuality audit of final outputs, and (iii) extend \sys to more product categories and retailers.
